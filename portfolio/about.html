Hi, I'm Frank

I've been designing digital products for nearly two decades, starting in graphic design before moving into product. Along the way I've helped build products at Red Bull, Headspace, Instagram, DoorDash, and now Anthropic.

Today at Anthropic, I design for Claude Code and work on Growth projects, shaping how people build and work alongside AI. Before that, I designed creator experiences at Instagram used by billions of people, worked on core product and growth at Headspace as it scaled into one of the world's leading wellness platforms, and helped launch Red Bull TV across twelve platforms as Red Bull grew into a global media company.

Outside of my day job, I've always been an entrepreneur at heart. I founded an agency, built communities like LA Design & Dev, run Sunshine Shop, and sing in a band called Monk. Those pursuits keep me curious, and remind me why I became a designer in the first place: to imagine new possibilities and bring them to life.

expertise

I love building brands and designing engaging experiences, digital and physical.

From promoting concerts to shaping products for global platforms, I’ve always been drawn to how design brings people together.

Whether I'm building from scratch or joining an established team, I look for the places where things can be better.

expertise

I bring an entrepreneurial spirit, cross-domain fluency, and a leadership style grounded in mentorship and heart.
