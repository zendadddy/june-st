Hi, I'm Frank

I've been designing digital products for nearly 20 years, starting in graphic design. Along the way I've built products at Red Bull, Headspace, Instagram, DoorDash, and now Anthropic.

Today I design for Claude Code and work on growth at Anthropic, shaping how people build with AI. Before that, I redesigned DoorDash's homepage and order flow, designed creator tools at Instagram used by billions, worked on core product and growth at Headspace, and helped launch Red Bull TV on twelve platforms.

Outside work, I'm an entrepreneur at heart. I founded an agency, built communities like Design & Dev — LA / SF / NYC, run Sunshine Shop, and sing in a band called Monk. They keep me curious, and remind me why I became a designer: to imagine new things and bring them to life.
